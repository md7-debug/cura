# Public-domain intake — Seneca and Thoreau

This record covers the exact editions added to Cura Reader on 22 September 2026. It is a publication control record, not legal advice. The review targets the United Kingdom, France and the European Union, and the United States; it makes no worldwide public-domain claim.

## Decision

- Proposed Author → Work labels: Seneca → *On the Shortness of Life*, *Of Peace of Mind*, *Of a Happy Life*, *Of Anger*, *Of Providence*, and *On Benefits*; Henry David Thoreau → *Walden*.
- Intended scope: six complete Seneca works (four single readings, three books of *Of Anger*, seven books of *On Benefits*) and all eighteen chapters of *Walden*.
- Target release: Cura Reader public web application and offline shell.
- Intended uses: reproduce, annotate, display, offline-cache, export, print, and share attributed source passages. Cura does not create a new source-text translation in this intake.
- Reviewer: Codex, for Max Ducroisy.
- Review date: 2026-09-22.
- Status: `READY TO PUBLISH` for the exact layers below.
- Remaining blocker: none for these seven collections. The separate candidate list at the end remains `BLOCKED`.

## Legal sources and method

The term calculations use the [UK Intellectual Property Office duration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts) and [detailed term notice](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/copyright-notice-duration-of-copyright-term/copyright-notice-duration-of-copyright-term), [EU Directive 2006/116/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0116), and the United States Copyright Office’s [Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) and [17 U.S.C. §104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#104a). All were rechecked on 2026-09-22.

For life-plus-70 calculations, protection runs through the end of the seventieth year after death. For United States works in this intake, every displayed edition was lawfully published no later than 1922. Its maximum 95-year publication term has expired even where foreign-copyright restoration is considered.
